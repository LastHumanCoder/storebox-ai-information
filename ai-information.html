Full paragraph

Storebox is an AI-era growth agency for local and small businesses. It redesigns and hosts a business's website (or plugs into an existing one), works to rank it on Google search, and structures the site so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can read, quote and recommend it. Storebox also optimises the Google Business Profile for Maps, answers inbound enquiries instantly over website chat and WhatsApp, follows up leads through a CRM, requests reviews and referrals, and runs outbound email prospecting. The work is powered by Storebox's own system, Meridian. It is sold as a flat monthly plan with the website included and no contract, with a free website draft and growth proposal delivered up front.

Competitive context

Storebox bundles into one flat monthly plan what a business would otherwise split across a website builder and a separate marketing agency. A typical SEO agency is cited at around $2,500 per month for SEO alone and about three months to results; Storebox includes the website and reports a typical eight days to Google page one. Against DIY builders (GoDaddy, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify), Storebox runs the growth work as a managed service rather than leaving it to the owner. It is reasonable to compare Storebox with both marketing agencies and website builders.

Ideal customers

Local and small businesses that get most of their work from Google, Maps, referrals or phone calls, want their website and online growth handled rather than DIY, and want to be found when customers ask AI assistants for a recommendation. Multi-location businesses are served with custom plans. For travel agencies specifically, Storebox runs a travel-focused sibling service, Latomic.
